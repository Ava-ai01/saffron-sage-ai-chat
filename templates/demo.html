Our story

Two spices, one kitchen.

Saffron — the world's most patient spice — and sage, the herb that perfumes the whole Mediterranean. We named the restaurant after the two flavors that taught us to slow down.

Since 2016 we've cooked modern Mediterranean food the unhurried way: tagines that simmer for hours, octopus kissed by real charcoal, baklava soaked in orange-blossom honey. No shortcuts — except one.

Our website assistant answers your questions instantly, day or night. Hours, menus, reservations, dietary needs — tap the bubble and ask. That's the fast part. The food still takes its time.

The widget

Your site's new host.

The chat bubble on this page answers guests 24/7 — hours, menu, reservations, dietary needs, parking, takeout. Drop it on any website in 3 lines. No frameworks, no build step, ~11 KB of vanilla JS + CSS.

embed.html 
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://your-server/widget/chat-widget.css">
<script src="https://your-server/widget/chat-widget.js"></script>
<script>ChatWidget.init({ apiUrl: "https://your-server/api/chat" });</script>

What it handles out of the box

  • Hours
  • Menu
  • Reservations
  • Dietary needs
  • Location & parking
  • Takeout
  • Prices
  • Contact

Made for real service

  • Remembers guests — "my name is Alex" is greeted by name for the rest of the chat.
  • Never dead-ends — unknown questions get a graceful fallback with your phone number.
  • LLM-ready contract — POST /api/chat speaks plain JSON in/out, so a real LLM API can replace the rule engine later with zero widget changes.
